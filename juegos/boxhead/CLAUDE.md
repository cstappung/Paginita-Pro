# Pro BoxHead — notes for Claude

Room game of Juegos. The shared architecture (move log, `reducir`, rooms, fin cartel, votes, sound) is in `colabtex/src/juegos/CLAUDE.md`; logros/coins/ranking wiring in `docs/claude/juegos/perfiles-y-economia.md`. The room postman is `colabtex/src/juegos/boxhead.js`.

**Pro BoxHead (id `boxhead`, renamed from «Boxhead»: the id stays, the rules,
rooms and tables use it) is a top-down shooter in an iframe, Yemas' sibling.**
An original recreation of *Boxhead: The 2Play Rooms* (its weapons, unlock
multipliers, enemies, skins and five maps, `juegos/boxhead/js/datos.js`, UMD
`BoxheadDatos`), but the room holds one to eight rather than two:
`JUEGOS.boxhead` has `minimo: 1, cupo: 8`, and `minimoDe` raises it to two for
`versus`. `juegos/boxhead/` is the document (`js/juego.js` the canvas game,
also playable alone as practice); `colabtex/src/juegos/boxhead.js`
(`crearBoxhead`) is the postman, on the same WebRTC mesh as Yemas
(`malla.js` + `crearDirecto`, mailbox `vivo/<pid>/rtc`). The first seat still
standing directs the enemies, as in Yemas' zombies. Things to keep:

- **The log carries only what decides the game**: `{t:"muere", uid, por, a,
  pts?, k?, n?}` written by whoever died and, in coop, `{t:"nivel", n}`
  written by the director when a level is cleared. `redBoxhead` accepts only
  the next level, and a new level revives the fallen. Coop ends when every
  player still in the room is down; the most points wins. Versus counts kills
  to `meta` (5/10/20, `BX_METAS`); a self-kill or a kill by someone gone counts
  a death and no kill.
- **Weapons unlock with the highest multiplier reached** (`ARMAS[i].mul`),
  never lost; ids 0–7 never renumber, 8 is a bite and 9 a devil's fireball
  (`BX_ARMAS`). Every other multiplier brings an **upgrade** (`MEJORAS`,
  generated by `generaMejoras` in `datos.js`, rotating over the weapons
  already unlocked through each weapon's `PASOS`, each stat capped by
  `tope`); `arma(id, mejoras)` is the weapon with them applied. When the
  combo bar empties the multiplier **drops by one** (`bajadaCombo`, a
  shorter bar each step) down to ×1, not all at once.
- **Pause stops everyone.** A paused player publishes `pz` in its state, and
  every frame (`quienPausa`) freezes `paso` while any player in the room has
  it — the director included, so enemies stop. It lifts itself after
  `PAUSA_MAX` (2 min). A spectator's pause is local.
- **Hits knock enemies back** (`danaEnemigo` with the source position:
  `aturd` 0.5 s and a decaying `kv`; the stun rides `zb` so the others draw
  the lean, `tb`). Devils' fireballs carry `de` and hurt any other enemy
  they cross: friendly fire.
- The HUD copies the original: points and multiplier centred at the top,
  the combo as a semicircle (`#marca`, an SVG arc with `pathLength`), and
  health, weapon and ammo drawn on the canvas over the player
  (`barraVida`). Muros/barrels travel with their upgraded `vida`/`d`.
- **Bosses** (`ENEMIGOS` 6–15, `MINIS`/`GRANDES`, `jefeDeNivel` in
  `datos.js`): a level ending in 5 adds a mini boss to the normal wave, a
  level ending in 0 brings a big boss **alone** (the director zeroes the
  queue, `dir.q = 0`); only what its ability summons comes with it. Five of
  each, rotating once all have appeared. They have `desde: Infinity`, so
  `mezclaNivel` never picks them. Each has one `hab` with cooldown `cd`,
  run by the director as a small state machine (`est`: 0 idle, 1 marking a
  shockwave, 2 charging, 3 executing, 4 faded, 5 furious) that rides `zb`
  so every screen draws the warning. `radio` is for walls (so they fit the
  corridors), `rg` for bullets and bites, `talla` for the drawing; their
  life is `vidaJefe` (level and players). Their bar is `#jefeBar`. Nothing
  new goes in the log, so no rules change.
- **Six enemy kinds** (`ENEMIGOS`, k 0–5: zombi, diablo, corredor, bruto,
  explosivo, escupidor), each entering at its `desde` level and growing its
  share by `parte` per level up to `tope` (`mezclaNivel`, zombies always
  ≥ 20 %). The explosivo detonates on contact or death (a queued explosion
  that hurts players and enemies); the escupidor's acid is a fireball whose
  `zb.f` entry carries the kind (`[id,x,y,vx,vy,k]`). **Difficulty is
  linear on purpose** (`totalNivel`, `vidaEnemigo`, `velEnemigo`,
  `golpeNivel`, `ritmoNivel`, `maxVivos`): it used to jump from easy to a
  wall around level 8–12, and the test pins every step as small.
- **Armour and the shop are each player's own**, never in the log: `$`
  cells in the map are supply booths where B/R/H/G buy ammo, armour (absorbs
  60 % of a hit, `ARMADURA`), a medkit and `potencia` (+10 % damage, five
  levels, `factorPotencia`) with points. A blue box drop gives armour. `pen`
  upgrades let pistol, uzi and shotgun pellets go through one more enemy each.
  `b` cells are map barrels that come back each level (`reponBarriles`).
- **The shop is a clickable panel** (`#tienda`, `pintaTienda`/`armaTienda` in
  `juego.js`), shown while `tiendaCerca()`: built once, then only classes and
  texts change (rebuilding it every 0.2 s ate clicks). Its buttons never take
  focus (`sinFoco`): Space shoots and would otherwise re-buy the last item.
  Keys still work (`TECLA_TIENDA` from `D.TIENDA`, plus **U** = upgrade the
  weapon in hand and **F** = place a muro reforzado).
- **Shop weapon levels** (`NIVEL_ARMA`, `precioNivelArma`, `conNivel` in
  `datos.js`): up to 5 per weapon, each `sube` (×1.8) dearer, applied on top
  of the multiplier upgrades — always read a weapon through `armaDe(i)`, never
  `D.arma(i, yo.mejoras)` alone. The level changes the look: `partesArma(w,
  nivel)` retints the metal (pulida 1–2, dorada 3–4, plasma 5 with a muzzle
  glow) and `iconoArma(..., nivel)` the HUD icon. The level in hand travels
  as `nv` in the player state so the others draw it; hits already carry the
  damage, so nothing else changes on the network.
- **Muro reforzado** (`blindado` in `TIENDA`, `MURO_BLINDADO`): bought per
  unit (`yo.bl`, max 6), placed with F as a `pon` request with a 7th field
  `bl: 1`. It is a `k: 'muro'` object with `bl` and `MURO_BLINDADO.vida`
  (1000, the director ignores the client's figure), so every rule about
  muros (rodeo, Demoledor, bites) applies unchanged. It travels as a 10th
  field of `zb.o`; an old client draws it as a plain muro.
- **Enemies go around the players' muros and barrels** (`calculaFlujo`:
  `flujoL` is the BFS with the cells those objects touch blocked, `tapadas`;
  `flujo` ignores them). An enemy uses `flujoL` while its cell has a distance
  there (`rodea`), never chases straight into an object (`objetoEnMedio`), and
  a graze is not an attack; only with no free path (or 1.5 s stuck against
  one, `e.tr`) does it bite muros and does an explosivo blow on one. **The
  Demoledor** (`ENEMIGOS[16]`, `rompe`, `dObj` ×4 damage to objects, from
  level 6, at most 5 %) always takes `flujo` and goes for the walls. New
  enemies are appended after the bosses: `PUNTOS`, `TALLA_Z`, `VIDA_Z` and
  `PAL_E` are indexed by `k`.
- **A body overlapping a map wall cannot move** (every step still collides):
  big ones spawning in a corner with the random offset stayed there forever.
  `desatasca` moves it to the nearest free spot, at spawn and every frame.
  `__boxhead.calma/ponYo/enemigo/pon/objetos` set up these cases in practice.
- **A muro placed on yourself no longer traps you**: `pon` refuses one that
  overlaps the player, and `choca` ignores an object the body already
  overlaps, so walking out of it is always possible.
- **What you see lives in `js/grafico.js`** (`BoxheadGrafico`), the sounds in
  `js/audio.js` (`BoxheadAudio`); `juego.js` only decides what to draw.
  The audio caps the pieces sounding at once (`carga`, `TOPE`/`TOPE_LEJOS`:
  distant sounds drop first) and ends in a limiter: with the horde on
  screen it used to crackle and go silent.
  Characters are **cubes in fake 3D**: `modelo` yaws each part to where the
  body faces, pitches it back (`cae`: the knockback lean, the death fall),
  projects with `KY` and paints only the faces toward the camera, sorted by
  depth. An animated part goes through `humanoide`'s parts list, never as
  extra canvas drawing, or it will not rotate. `PAL_E` has **one palette per
  `ENEMIGOS` entry** (the test pins it): a new enemy needs its palette.
  Models draw at `ESC` (1.15) over their collision radius; bars and labels
  above heads are placed for that scale.
- **The floor keeps everything** (`crearSuelo`): one canvas of the whole map,
  painted once per map theme (`TEMAS`, keyed by map id) plus a static shadow
  layer, where every blood drop, pool and scorch is stamped and **never
  erased** during a game. Blood comes from particles that stamp where they
  land (`fx.sangre`), so a new effect that should stain must do the same.
  Walls are white chamfered blocks (`bloque`, `muroDe(mapa)` tints them);
  neighbour walls merge, so do not stroke shared edges.
- **A death travels as `[id, quien, k, x, y, ang, tipo]`** in `zb.m`: `ang`
  (centiradians) is where the hit threw it, `tipo` 0 falls (`fx.cadaver`,
  which leaves its pool), 1 bursts (explosive weapon), 2 the explosivo
  blowing up. Old clients send five fields; `procesaMuerte` falls back to a
  random angle. Knockback depends on the weapon (`EMPUJE_ARMA`) and adds up
  while stunned, capped by mass: that is what makes the shotgun hit hard.
- Room options: `variante` (coop/versus), `mapa` (`BX_MAPAS`, must match
  `ORDEN_MAPAS`) and `meta` — not `modo`. `'boxhead'` was added to the
  rules' `juego` and `logros` whitelists, so they must be re-published.
  `tests/boxhead.test.cjs` covers the reducer and walks every map.

## Phase 1 of the mega update (`boxhead-9`): controls, economy, feel, HUD, music

- **Chatarra is the shop currency; points only go up.** `yo.chat` is local, never
  in the log or the player state, like armour. My kills drop it (`miBaja` →
  `sueltaChatarra`, `D.chatarraDe(k)` = 1.5 × base points, no multiplier) as
  pieces in `CH` that fly to me (`pasoChatarra`); dying cashes what is left
  (`cobra`). `compra`/`subeArma` charge `yo.chat`, never `yo.pts`: points decide
  the coop winner, the room table and logros. `D.precioTienda(id, pot, nivel)`
  raises supplies 5 % per level (cap ×2.5); potencia and weapon levels are fixed.
- **Controls.** Shift / LT / the touch «Fijar» lock the facing (`yo.fija`, an
  arrow on the floor, 0.88 speed). `Mando.configura` has `stick: null` and
  `flechas: false`: the game reads the sticks itself (`leeMando`, only when
  `modo === 'juego'`) with `D.dirStick` (8 directions with hysteresis; tested).
  Light tilt turns without moving; the right stick looks without moving. The
  d-pad buys at a `$` (`MANDO_TIENDA` maps items to buttons, and the shop shows
  their glyphs) and cycles weapons away from it. Vibration (`vibra`) lives here,
  not in `mando.js`, so the shared module (34 pages) did not change. Touch
  controls (`#tactil`, shown with `html.tactil` on the first touch or a coarse
  pointer) feed the same stick path; the header says `@controles: tactil teclado`.
- **mando.js puts its hints next to `#ayuda` without the id**, so
  `#hud>.mando-pista` must carry the position. Without it they landed on top of
  `#info`.
- **Hit-stop** (`impacto`, max 0.1 s) only scales this screen's `dt` (×0.08 in
  `cuadro`): boss death, big explosion nearby, point-blank shotgun. Nothing may
  depend on it, and it must stay short because a director's freeze is everyone's.
- **HUD.** Rewards show at the same time (`PREMIOS`, at most 3, 2.2 s each) and
  weapon upgrades merge into one line. A queue used to delay a box's «+50 vida»
  by tens of seconds. Tips (`CONSEJOS`) show once each (`boxhead.consejos`). The
  shop sits on the right side (on phones under the score). The combo arc blinks
  under 25 % (`#marca.cae`, `avisoCombo`), and milestones ×10/25/50/100…
  (`HITOS`) pulse the camera. The end screen shows `yo.st` (shots and hits per
  pellet, kills per weapon, bosses). Options live in the pause and are stored as
  `boxhead.*` keys.
- **Music** (`audio.js`, `musica(meta, jefe)`): a 16th-note sequencer through
  its own `mus.bus` into the compressor (`salida`), so `volumen.js` still governs
  it. `juego.js` sends the intensity from enemies and the multiplier, −1 to fade
  out, and a live boss switches the theme. Music pieces pass `libre` so they never
  count in `carga`. Otherwise the horde's cap would mute the effects.
- **Recorded effects:** the loader is ready (`MUESTRAS`/`GAN`/`muestra`), but
  `MUESTRAS` stays empty until the ElevenLabs files exist as
  `sfx/<key>-<n>.mp3`. Each file's `GAN` must be measured against the
  synthesized sound. The `switch` in `son` remains the fallback.


## Phase 2.1: deterministic practice engine (`boxhead-10`)

- **Practice is replayable.** Gameplay randomness goes through `rn`/`rz`, a
  mulberry32 seeded per game in `practica(semilla)`; `azar` (Math.random) is only
  for what is seen. A new random call that changes the game must use `rn`/`rz`,
  or proofs stop replaying. Rooms keep `rng = Math.random` (the director rules).
  `dist`/`hyp` use `Math.sqrt`, never `Math.hypot`. Trig still differs in the
  last bit between engines (same risk PRO DEFENDER took). In practice `nid()` is a
  counter, and `practica` resets everything a sim carries across games
  (`vistosM`, `marca`, `CH`…). A seen-id set left full silently ate kills.
- **Fixed steps.** `cuadro` advances practice in steps of `PASO` (1/60 s, at
  most 4 per frame); `pasoN` counts them (incremented at the end of `paso`, never
  while paused). Online still uses a variable `dt`. No `setTimeout` in the sim:
  the empty-weapon switch became `yo.vacio`.
- **The recorder** (`graba`): `[paso, 0, mov, vel·20, mira+1, fija|disp<<1]`
  when the stick or keys change (`entradaPaso`, speed in twentieths), and
  `[paso, code, arg]` for each discrete action, which must go through
  `accion(code, arg)` (1 cycle, 2 pick, 3 detonate, 4 reinforced wall, 5 weapon
  level, 6 buy item i of `D.TIENDA`). Internal calls (`dispara` → `eligeArma(0)`)
  stay direct. The proof is `{v: 1, ve: VE, u, m, s, j, k}` (`__boxhead.prueba()`),
  with `k` = the step of `finPractica`. **Bump `VE` whenever the simulation
  changes**: an old proof would replay to a different end.
- **`__boxhead.rehace(p)`** replays it without a screen: `js/sin-pantalla.js`
  turns DOM, canvas, audio and grafico into an inert proxy, so `datos.js` +
  `juego.js` run in a worker or in Node (`tests/boxhead.test.cjs` plays a bot
  through `__boxhead.entrada`, which records like real input, and replays in
  another context). About 0.1 ms per step.

## Phase 2.3: in the Solo Club (`boxhead-11`, card `boxheadclub`)

- **Same game, `?club=1`.** `solo/club.js` opens `juegos/boxhead/index.html?club=1`
  (not `juegos/club/<id>/`), and the head of `index.html` loads `conexion.js` only
  then. The ranking panel goes in the menu's `#clubTabla.shell`. The frame is a
  fixed 720 px; `alto` is ignored. Club key `boxheadclub` (as with
  tetris→tetrisclub): route `#solo/boxhead`, categories `club-boxhead-<map>`.
- **One table per map, ranked by highest level** (the user's call): `puntos` = the
  level you died on, `tiempo` = ms of play until you **reached** that level
  (`pasoNivel`, set in `simDirector`), not until death: equal levels rank by who got
  there first. Cap 999 (`club-datos.js` and the rules).
- **The account is in the seed** (`practica(s, grabar, cuenta)`, `?cuenta=` from
  the club frame, FNV-1a mixed in). A proof copied to another account replays
  differently. `rehace` rejects a proof without `u`.
- **Verifier** `solo/verifica/boxhead.js` replays in a Worker
  (`js/verificador.js`, which `importScripts` the game) or in Node through
  `globalThis.BoxheadRehace` (`js/rehace-node.cjs`). Level, final step and time
  must match exactly. **Its `RUTA` and the worker's imports carry `?v=boxhead-N`:
  bump them along with the page**, or a cached old engine rejects new proofs.
- The old `bots-boxhead` practice card is gone: the club card replaces
  it in `SOLOS`.

## Phase 2.2: upgrades to choose (`boxhead-12`, proof `ve: 2`)

- **1 of 3 at the start of each level from 2** (`alEmpezarNivel`, from the
  `nivelVisto` change in `paso`, so it fires solo and online alike). The 24 are
  `D.VENTAJAS` (id, `rar` 0–2, `max`, `equipo`); `D.sorteaVentajas(azar, …)` is
  pure and gets `rn`, so practice draws come from the seed. Their effects live in
  juego.js behind `vt(id)`; grep `vt('` to find them all. `hpMax()` replaced the
  literal 100.
- **The pick is action 7** (`accion(7, i)`: 1/2/3, ← ↑ → on the pad, a click).
  With no answer, `eligeVentaja(0)` runs **inside the sim** after
  `D.ESPERA_VENTAJA` s, so a replay does the same without it being recorded.
  In practice `simDirector` holds the next horde until the pick; online nobody
  waits. Digits 1–3 pick an upgrade instead of a weapon while the panel is open.
- Upgrade explosions (bala ígnea, estallido) call `explota(…, amigo = true)`:
  they never hurt you or your allies. Estallido is queued in `yo.booms` and
  fired in the next `pasoYo`, never inside the kill that caused it. The drone
  fires from the player's position; only the drawing hovers.
- `practica` resets `eleccion`. Any change to an upgrade's numbers changes the
  sim: **bump `VE`**.
